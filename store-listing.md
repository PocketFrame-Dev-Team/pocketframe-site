# Store listing copy

Text for the Google Play and App Store listings. Both stores have character limits, noted
beside each field. Written in the same plain voice as the app, for the same audience.

## App name (30 characters on both stores)

PocketFrame

## Subtitle / short description

**App Store subtitle (30 characters):**
Turn your phone into a frame

**Google Play short description (80 characters):**
Turn your phone into a digital picture frame. Your photos, playing on a loop.

## Full description (4,000 characters on both stores)

PocketFrame turns your phone into a digital picture frame.

Choose the photos you love, give the slideshow a name, and set your phone down. The photos
fade gently from one to the next and keep playing for as long as you like. Put it on a desk, a
bedside table, or the kitchen counter, and your favourite moments are always in view.

MADE TO BE EASY
• Big buttons and plain words, not mystery icons
• Text that grows with your phone's own text size
• Nothing that times out or disappears before you have read it
• Works in light and dark, whichever you prefer

YOUR SLIDESHOWS, YOUR WAY
• Make as many slideshows as you like
• Choose how long each photo stays on screen
• Shuffle, or play in the order you set
• Turn the phone sideways and the photos follow

PLAYS THE WAY A FRAME SHOULD
• The screen stays on while it plays
• A sleep timer stops it when you want
• Dim the screen for the evening
• Black and white, for a classic look

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Your photos are stored privately and only you can see them
• Sign in with your email, Google, Facebook or Apple, and your slideshows follow you to a new phone
• No ads, no tracking, nothing sold
• Delete your account, and everything in it, whenever you like

PocketFrame is free to use.

Questions? Email pocketframedev@gmail.com.
